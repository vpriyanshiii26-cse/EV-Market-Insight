# Phase 7: Project Documentation

## 1. Project Overview & Scope
The **Electric Vehicle Market Performance & Insights** project delivers an end-to-end business intelligence pipeline analyzing global electric mobility adoption. By transforming 200,000 raw vehicle records into accessible visual intelligence, the platform enables corporate executives, product designers, and sustainability directors to identify market gaps, evaluate battery efficiencies, and drive strategic growth.

## 2. Key Insights Derived
1. **Category Dominance:** Hatchbacks and compact electric two-wheelers represent the highest volume demand across urban commuter markets.
2. **Battery Chemistry Evolution:** NMC and LFP batteries account for the overwhelming majority of market share, with LFP dominating cost-sensitive compact segments due to superior cycle life.
3. **Customer Satisfaction Drivers:** Models offering driving ranges exceeding 320 KM and rapid DC fast-charging capabilities consistently achieved customer review scores of 4.5/5 or higher.
4. **Geographic Concentration:** Global production remains concentrated in three major continental economic hubs, underscoring the necessity of localized supply chains.

## 3. Advantages & Limitations
- **Advantages:** Real-time multi-variable filtering, intuitive visualization for non-technical stakeholders, modular architecture supporting horizontal data scaling.
- **Limitations:** Relies on historical periodic batch updates rather than real-time telemetry streaming from connected vehicle APIs.
